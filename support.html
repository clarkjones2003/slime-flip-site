Tips

  • Win by standing up. The slime has to land upright on the swirl, not lying down.
  • Hint shows your next few landings. Tap it again to hide them.
  • Hold a finger on the board to preview where each swipe would land: gold means standing, red means you'd fall.
  • Undo glows gold when you're stuck, and tells you how many moves to go back.
  • Prefer buttons to swipes? Turn on the arrow pad in Settings.
