SLIME FLIP

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version: Slime Flip collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

Slime Flip ("we", "us") is an iPhone game made by Clark Jones, an independent developer in Winter Park, Florida, USA. Contact: senoj2134789@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Slime Flip does not ask for your name, email or any account, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on your phone

  • Your level progress, stars, collected drops and the hats you've unlocked.
  • Your settings, such as sound, haptics and the on-screen arrow pad.

This is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Slime Flip is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example if a future version adds online features), we'll update the date above and describe the change here before it takes effect.
